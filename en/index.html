Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This is a translation of the Spanish privacy policy. If the two versions differ, the Spanish version prevails.

This policy explains what personal data Vayd processes when you use the app, what we use it for, who we share it with and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Roger Nomen Giner, tax ID (NIF) 47869172P, with address at C/Lilium 8, 43717 La Bisbal del Penedès, Spain. You can write to us about any privacy matter at rogernomen@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the Vayd app for iOS and Android and its associated services (your user account and the syncing of your collection). It doesn't apply to third-party sites or services the app may link to, such as game stores, which have their own policies.

2. What data we collect

We only collect the data Vayd needs to work. We don't ask for your location or your contacts.

Type of data Examples When we collect it
Account data Email, name, username (a random one at first, which you can change), password (stored encrypted, never in plain text), the app style you choose and the date of your last activity When you create your account
Sign in with Apple or Google The account identifier, email (or the private email Apple generates) and the name on that account, if it shares it If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google
Your collection Games, platform, format (physical or digital), status (not started, started, finished or dropped) and the dates you finish or drop them, hours and percentage you enter, sessions you time, wishlist and the games you choose for “What are we playing?” When you add or edit games
Ratings and reviews Your rating out of 10, your ratings by category (gameplay, graphics…), what you write when you finish or drop a game and the language it's written in, which we detect automatically on our server If you rate or review a game
Import from Xbox Your Xbox identifier (XUID) and gamertag, your games, hours played, achievements, the last time you played each game and the access keys Microsoft provides to keep them up to date, stored encrypted. We never see your Microsoft password Only if you connect your Xbox account
Import from Steam Your Steam identifier and profile name, your games (including those you haven't played), hours played, achievements and the last time you played each game. We never see your Steam password Only if you connect your Steam account
Playtime history The total hours of each game imported from Xbox or Steam at different points in time, with the date, platform and store. We save a record the first time we receive a game's hours and every time they change Only if you connect your Xbox or Steam account
Notifications Device token to send you alerts If you agree to receive notifications
Subscription Whether your premium plan is active and its renewal date. We never see your card details: payments are handled by Apple or Google If you subscribe
Technical data Device model, system and app version, app language, IP address and error logs When you use the app

Guest mode. If you explore Vayd without an account, we don't save any collection or account data: to save your games you need to sign in. We only process the technical data of the requests the app makes.

On your device. The app stores your session (encrypted), and the language and style you choose on your device so it doesn't have to ask you every time. Your session and style are deleted when you sign out, and everything is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Error monitoring. We use New Relic to detect errors and monitor the stability of the app and our servers. It collects technical data: device model, system and app version, IP address, error logs and response times. We don't send New Relic your email or the contents of your collection, and we don't use it for advertising or to track you across apps.

3. What we use your data for

We use your data to provide the service you ask for and to keep the app secure. We don't sell your data or use it to show you personalized advertising.

Keeping a history of your playtime so we can show your activity over time, for example how much you played each month. This feature isn't available in the app yet; we save the history from now on because it can't be recovered later Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), when you connect your account
Managing your premium subscription Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
Detecting errors, monitoring the stability of the app and servers (with New Relic), preventing abuse and protecting the security of the service Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
Answering your questions and requests Performance of a contract or, if you're not a user, legitimate interest
Complying with legal obligations, such as requests from the authorities Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c))

We don't make automated decisions with legal effects on you or build profiles for that purpose. The app's recommendations only suggest what to play.

Transfers outside the EU. Some of these providers are in the United States. In those cases, the transfer is covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, when the provider has joined it, or by the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards at rogernomen@gmail.com.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete it, we erase your data within 30 days at most.

Data Period
Account, collection, ratings and reviews While you have your account; erased no later than 30 days after you delete it
Backups Automatically overwritten within 7 days at most
Technical and error logs 30 days
Notification token Until you turn off notifications, sign out or delete your account
Connection with Xbox or Steam (identifier, name and access keys) Until you disconnect that account or delete your Vayd account
Games imported from Xbox or Steam When you disconnect, you choose whether to keep them in your collection or delete them. If you keep them, they stop updating and are kept until you remove them or delete your account
Playtime history While you have your account. A game's history is deleted when you remove it from your collection, and the Xbox or Steam history if you choose to delete their games when disconnecting. If you keep them, their history is also kept, but stops growing
Inactive accounts We'll let you know before deleting an account that has been inactive for 24 months

The average community rating is calculated without identifying you, so it may still include your rating anonymously after you delete your account. However, you stop counting among the users playing a game as soon as you remove it from your “Playing now” slot or delete your account. We may also keep some data blocked for the period required by law, only to deal with possible liabilities.

How we protect your data. Communications between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords and Xbox access keys are stored encrypted, the database can only be accessed from our API and we limit internal access to what's strictly necessary. No system is foolproof: if a security breach affected your data, we would let you know and notify the Spanish Data Protection Agency when the law requires it.

6. Your rights

You can exercise these rights over your data at any time:

  • Access: find out what data of yours we process.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data. You can edit almost all of it yourself in the app, including your username.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data. You can delete your account directly from Profile → Delete account.
  • Objection and restriction: object to processing based on legitimate interest or ask us to restrict its use.
  • Portability: receive your collection in a commonly used format (CSV) to take it to another service.
  • Withdraw your consent: turn off notifications or disconnect Xbox or Steam whenever you want, without affecting what was processed before.

Permissions on Microsoft and Steam. When you disconnect Xbox or delete your account, we delete our access keys and stop reading your data, but Vayd may keep appearing in your Microsoft account's authorized apps until you remove it there. Steam doesn't give us access keys: we only use your identifier and what your profile shows publicly.

To exercise them, write to rogernomen@gmail.com from your account's email. We'll answer within one month. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or with the data protection authority of your country.

7. Minors

Vayd is intended for people aged 14 or over, the age from which Spanish law allows you to give your own consent to the processing of your data. If you're under 14, you need permission from your parent or guardian. If we learn that we've collected data from someone under 14 without that permission, we'll delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or in the law. If the change is significant, we'll let you know in the app before it takes effect. The date of the last update always appears at the top.
