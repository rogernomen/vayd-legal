Delete your Vayd account

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in Vayd, the app for iOS and Android developed by Roger Nomen Giner, and the data linked to it.

1. Delete it from the app

  1. Open Vayd and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap the gear icon (Settings) at the top right.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm when the app asks you to.

Your account is closed and we start erasing your data straight away.

2. If you can't open the app

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, write to rogernomen@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my Vayd account". We will reply within one month, as set out in our privacy policy.

3. What we delete

  • Your account and profile (name, username, email, photo link, style and visibility settings).
  • Your collection: games, statuses, ratings, reviews and notes, including the ratings by category.
  • Your lists, tier lists, shelves and the games you picked as best and worst of the year.
  • Your followers and the accounts you follow.
  • The comments you posted and the reports you made.
  • Your connections with Xbox, Steam or PlayStation, including the access keys we stored, and your playtime history.
  • Your notification token.

4. What we keep and for how long

  • Your data: erased no later than 30 days after you delete your account.
  • Backups: automatically overwritten within 7 days at most.
  • Technical and error logs: kept for 30 days. They don't include the content of your collection.

Outside Vayd. Deleting your account removes our access keys and we stop reading your data, but Vayd can still appear among the authorized apps in your Microsoft account until you remove it there.

5. Contact

For any question about deleting your account or your data, write to rogernomen@gmail.com.
